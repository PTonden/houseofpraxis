The working studio of Paul Tonden, MBA

House of Praxis is built on a simple conviction: ideas earn their value in application. It is the home of a connected body of work in human-centered leadership during technological disruption, where doctoral research, an operating business, and two decades of leadership development practice feed one another.

The research studies how leaders hold onto human values when they adopt powerful new tools. The business tests those ideas against payroll, clients, and real consequences. The teaching carries what survives back to leaders and organizations.

Research

Doctoral candidate in Strategy & Innovation, studying how organizational leaders understand and enact human-centered values, including creativity, learning, and well-being, when integrating generative AI in the workplace. DBA expected 2027.

Venture

Blue Moon Estate Sales Orlando West serves families navigating life transitions: estate sales, downsizing, and move management. The company runs on an AI-augmented operating model designed and built in-house, a living case study in what the research examines.

Practice

Executive education, leadership development, and human-centered AI workshops for organizations, delivered through Paint It Black Design Group. Maxwell Leadership Certified Coach, Speaker & Trainer; HBDI and DiSC certified practitioner.

Conversation

Thought Partners Podcast, co-hosted ongoing conversations on leadership in practice, franchise ownership, and the intersection of strategy and human-centered design.
