Revisited: what is a thought partner?

Ed Buchanan and I recorded our first Thought Partners conversation in October 2025, trying to pin down what a thought partner actually is and how it differs from a mentor, a coach, or a manager. On December 1 it comes back as the first episode of Thought Partners Revisited, with a new intro and a tighter edit.

Listening again, the part that holds up for me is the shape we landed on: a thought partner challenges, clarifies, and co-creates, and wants the other person to succeed for their own sake. Along the way we wandered through the trust equation, Lizzie Magie's lost "prosperity" version of Monopoly, and the Johari window.

The episode ends with an experiment worth trying this week. Have a ten-minute conversation that is entirely about the other person. Ask who, what, and how. Leave out why, which tends to put people on the defensive.
